# Charminar Airways — Check-in and Boarding

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Airport Operations.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Web check-in

Web check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure. Customers
can check in on the website or app and download their boarding pass, or show it on their phone.
Seats not chosen before check-in are assigned for free.

## Airport check-in

Airport check-in counters close:

- 45 minutes before departure for domestic flights.
- 75 minutes before departure for international flights.

Customers who reach the counter after these times cannot be checked in. Domestic passengers
should arrive at the airport at least 2 hours before departure.

## Boarding

Boarding gates close 25 minutes before departure. Passengers who are not at the gate by then
lose their seat and are treated as no-shows.

## Identity documents

Domestic passengers must show a valid government photo ID at the airport entrance and at
check-in. We accept Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving licence or PAN card. DigiLocker copies
are accepted. International passengers need a passport and any visa required for their
destination.
