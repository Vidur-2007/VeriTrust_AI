# Charminar Airways — Cancellations Manual

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Revenue Management.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Free look-in period

Within 48 hours of booking, a domestic ticket can be cancelled or changed free of charge, as
long as departure is at least 7 days away. After 48 hours, or for flights less than 7 days
away, the normal fees apply.

## Domestic cancellation fees

- More than 72 hours before departure: ₹3,500 per passenger per sector.
- Within 72 hours of departure: ₹4,250 per passenger per sector.

Domestic bookings can be cancelled up to 2 hours before departure. After that, the ticket is
treated as a no-show; see the Refunds guide.

## International cancellation fees

Cancelling an international booking costs ₹6,000 per passenger per sector.

## Fee cap

The cancellation fee never exceeds the base fare plus fuel surcharge of the ticket. On very low
fares the customer therefore always gets back at least the taxes.

## When the airline cancels

If Charminar Airways cancels a flight with less than 24 hours' notice, passengers receive
compensation of up to ₹10,000, depending on flight duration, in addition to a full refund.
Compensation is not paid when the cancellation is caused by weather or other events beyond our
control. Agents must not promise a specific compensation amount; the Customer Accounts team
confirms it.
