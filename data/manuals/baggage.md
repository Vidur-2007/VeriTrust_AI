# Charminar Airways — Baggage Handbook

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Ground Services, Hyderabad hub.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Cabin baggage

Every passenger may bring one cabin bag weighing up to 7 kg on all Charminar Airways flights,
domestic and international. The bag must fit in the overhead bin and must not exceed
55 cm x 35 cm x 25 cm, including wheels and handles. Bags that are too large or too heavy at
the gate will be moved to the hold and excess baggage charges may apply.

Alongside the cabin bag, passengers may carry one personal item such as a handbag, laptop bag
or small backpack weighing up to 3 kg. It must fit under the seat in front.

## Checked baggage allowance

The free checked baggage allowance depends on the fare type:

- Domestic Saver fare: 15 kg per passenger.
- Domestic Flex fare: 20 kg per passenger.
- International economy: 30 kg per passenger.

The allowance may be split across several pieces, but no single piece may weigh more than
32 kg. This limit exists for the safety of our baggage handlers and cannot be waived, even on
payment. Heavier items must be sent as cargo.

Charminar Miles Gold members receive extra allowance; see the Loyalty manual.

## Excess baggage at the airport

Baggage above the free allowance is charged at the check-in counter:

- Domestic flights: ₹550 per kg.
- International flights: ₹1,500 per kg.

Payment is accepted by card or UPI at the counter. Agents should always point customers to
prepaid excess baggage, which is cheaper.

## Prepaid excess baggage

Customers who know they will carry more can buy excess baggage in advance on the website,
app or through the call centre. A prepaid 5 kg block on domestic flights costs ₹2,750 when it
is bought at least 24 hours before departure. After that cutoff, only the airport rate applies.

## Sports equipment

Golf bags, bicycles, surfboards and similar items are accepted as checked baggage. On domestic
flights a handling fee of ₹2,500 per item applies in addition to any excess weight charges.
