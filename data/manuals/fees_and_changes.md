# Charminar Airways — Fees and Booking Changes

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Revenue Management.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Changing the date or time of a booking

Customers can change the date or time of their flight on the website, app, or through the
call centre.

- Domestic bookings: a change fee of ₹2,500 per passenger per sector.
- International bookings: a change fee of ₹4,500 per passenger per sector.

In both cases the customer also pays the fare difference if the new fare is higher. If the new
fare is lower, the difference is not refunded. Changes made within the free look-in period are
free; see the Cancellations manual.

## Same-day change to an earlier flight

At the airport, a customer may move to an earlier Charminar Airways flight on the same day for
₹2,000 per passenger, subject to seat availability. This is only available at the airport
counter, not online.

## Name corrections

Misspelt names can be corrected for ₹1,000 per passenger. A ticket can never be transferred to
a different person. Agents must check that the corrected name matches the passenger's ID.

## Seats

- Standard seat selection on domestic flights starts at ₹250 per seat per sector.
- Extra legroom seats on domestic flights cost ₹1,200 per seat per sector.

## Booking fees

- Website and app bookings carry a convenience fee of ₹350 per passenger per sector.
- Tickets bought at an airport ticket counter carry a service fee of ₹500 per passenger.

These fees are shown before payment. The convenience fee is refunded only when the airline
cancels the flight.
