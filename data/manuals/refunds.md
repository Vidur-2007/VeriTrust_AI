# Charminar Airways — Refunds Guide

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Customer Accounts.
Last revised: March 2026.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds always go back to the original form of payment. For online bookings we never give
refunds in cash. Refunds to credit cards, debit cards and UPI are processed within 7 working
days of the cancellation.
## Credit shell

Instead of a refund, a customer may choose a credit shell, which can be used for a future
booking. A credit shell is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued. It can be used in
part, and the remaining balance stays valid until the same expiry date.

## No-shows

If a customer does not turn up for their flight, the base fare is not refundable. Only
statutory taxes and airport charges are refunded. The customer must ask for this refund within
90 days of the scheduled travel date; after 90 days nothing can be refunded.

## When Charminar Airways cancels a flight

If we cancel a flight, the customer may choose a 100% refund of the total amount paid,
including the convenience fee, or a free change to another flight. For compensation on
short-notice cancellations, see the Cancellations manual.

## Long delays

If a flight is delayed by more than 6 hours, the customer may cancel and receive a full refund.
For shorter delays the normal cancellation fee applies.

## Travel agent bookings

If the ticket was bought through a travel agent, the refund can only be processed through that
agent. Agents should politely direct the customer to their travel agent and must not promise a
timeline for agent-processed refunds.
