# Charminar Airways — Loyalty, Special Assistance and Pets

Internal customer support manual. Document owners: Loyalty Team, Customer Care.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Charminar Miles: earning

Charminar Miles members earn 5 Miles for every ₹100 of base fare. Taxes and fees do not earn
Miles. Silver members earn a 25% bonus on every eligible flight. Miles are credited within
7 days of travel.

## Charminar Miles: tiers

- Blue: on joining.
- Silver: 15,000 Tier Miles earned within 12 months.
- Gold: 40,000 Tier Miles earned within 12 months.

Gold members receive an extra 10 kg of checked baggage allowance on every flight, plus priority
check-in and boarding.

## Charminar Miles: redeeming and expiry

The minimum redemption is 1,000 Miles. Miles can be used for flights, seats and excess baggage.
Miles expire 36 months after the month in which they were earned.

## Wheelchair and mobility assistance

Wheelchair assistance is free of charge. Customers should request it at least 48 hours before
departure, through the website, app or call centre. Wheelchairs and other mobility aids are
carried free of charge in addition to the normal baggage allowance.

## Unaccompanied minors

Children aged 5 to 11 travelling alone must use the unaccompanied minor service. The fee is
₹4,000 per child per sector. A staff member stays with the child from check-in until they are
handed over to the named adult at arrival, who must show photo ID.

## Pregnancy

Pregnant passengers need a fitness-to-fly certificate from a doctor from the 28th week of
pregnancy. We cannot accept passengers beyond the 36th week of a single pregnancy.

## Pets in the cabin

Pets are allowed in the cabin on domestic flights only. The pet must weigh no more than 7 kg
including its carrier, and the carrier must fit under the seat. The fee is ₹3,500 per pet per
sector. A maximum of 2 pets are accepted in the cabin on any one flight, on a first-come basis,
so customers should book early through the call centre.

## Pets in the hold

Larger pets travel in the hold, in an IATA-approved crate. The charge is ₹1,000 per kg of the
combined weight of pet and crate. Snub-nosed breeds such as pugs, bulldogs and Persian cats are
not accepted in the hold, because they are at higher risk of breathing problems.

## Pet age and documents

Pets must be at least 8 weeks old to travel and need a vaccination record and a health
certificate issued within 7 days of travel.
