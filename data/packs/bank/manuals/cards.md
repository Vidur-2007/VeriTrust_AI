# Golconda Bank — Debit and Credit Cards

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Cards and Payments.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Classic debit card

- Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit: ₹50,000.
- Daily limit for shop and online purchases: ₹75,000.
- Fee: free in the first year, then ₹200 per year.

## Platinum debit card

- Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit: ₹80,000.
- Fee: ₹750 per year.

## Lost, stolen or damaged cards

A customer can block a card at once in the mobile app or through the 24-hour helpline.
Replacing a lost or damaged debit card costs ₹250.

A customer who reports an unauthorised card transaction within 3 working days has zero
liability. Agents must log the report time exactly as the customer states it.

## Using a card abroad

International usage is switched off by default on all debit cards. The customer can switch it
on in the mobile app. Card transactions in a foreign currency carry a markup of 3.5%.

## Silver credit card

- Interest-free period: up to 48 days on purchases.
- Interest: 3.25% per month on any unpaid balance.
- Cash advance: 2.5% of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum of ₹500. Interest on a cash advance
  starts on the day of withdrawal.

Agents must never ask for a PIN, OTP, CVV or full card number.
