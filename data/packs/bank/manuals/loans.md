# Golconda Bank — Loans

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Retail Assets.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Home loans

Home loan interest rates start at 8.40% per year, on a floating rate. The rate offered depends
on the customer's credit score and the loan amount.

- Processing fee: 0.50% of the loan amount, up to a maximum of ₹15,000.
- Maximum tenure: 30 years.
- Prepayment: there is no prepayment charge on floating-rate home loans.

## Personal loans

Personal loan interest rates start at 11.50% per year.

- Processing fee: 2% of the loan amount.
- Maximum tenure: 5 years.
- Prepayment: 3% of the outstanding principal. Prepayment is not allowed in the first 6 months.

## Car loans

Car loan interest rates start at 9.25% per year, for a tenure of up to 7 years.

## Missed EMIs

A missed or bounced EMI is charged ₹500 each time. Agents cannot waive this charge; requests for
a waiver go to the branch manager.

Support agents must never tell a customer that a loan is approved. Approval is confirmed only
in the sanction letter.
