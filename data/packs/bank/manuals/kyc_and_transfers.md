# Golconda Bank — KYC and Money Transfers

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Compliance and Digital Banking.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Opening an account

Opening an account needs a PAN and an Aadhaar card, or another officially valid identity and
address document. A minor aged 10 years or older can open and operate a savings account on
their own.

## Re-KYC

Every customer must update KYC from time to time. How often depends on the risk category:

- High-risk customers: every 2 years.
- Medium-risk customers: every 8 years.
- Low-risk customers: every 10 years.

Video KYC is available from 9 am to 6 pm on bank working days. If re-KYC is overdue, the account
is put on a debit freeze after 30 days' notice. No customer is exempt from re-KYC.

## Change of address

A customer must tell the bank about a change of address within 30 days, with proof of the new
address.

## UPI, IMPS, NEFT and RTGS

- UPI: transfers are limited to ₹75,000 per day.
- IMPS: ₹5 per transfer.
- NEFT: free in the mobile app and net banking; ₹15 per transfer at a branch.
- RTGS: for transfers of ₹2,00,000 or more.

## New beneficiaries

For 24 hours after a new beneficiary is added, transfers to that beneficiary are limited to
₹50,000 in total.

## Standing instructions

A standing instruction that fails because of low balance is charged ₹150.
