# Golconda Bank — Savings Accounts and Service Charges

Internal customer support manual. Document owner: Retail Liabilities.
Last revised: March 2026.

## Minimum balance

A Regular Savings account must keep an average monthly balance:

- Metro and urban branches: ₹5,000.
- Rural branches: ₹2,000.

If the minimum average balance is not kept, a non-maintenance charge of ₹200 per quarter
applies. The charge is debited in the first week of the next quarter.

## Basic Savings account

The Basic Savings account has no minimum balance. It allows 4 withdrawals per month, counting
ATM, branch and transfer withdrawals together. There is no non-maintenance charge on this
account.

## Interest

Savings accounts earn interest at 3% per year. Interest is worked out on the daily closing
balance and credited every quarter.

## ATM transactions

- At Golconda Bank ATMs: 5 free transactions per month.
- At other banks' ATMs: 3 free transactions per month.

Transactions beyond the free monthly limit cost ₹21 each. Balance enquiries and mini
statements count as transactions.

## Cheque books and passbooks

The first 20 cheque leaves each year are free. Additional leaves cost ₹4 per leaf. A duplicate
passbook costs ₹100.

## Alerts

SMS alerts cost ₹15 per quarter. Email alerts and app notifications are free.

## Closing an account

Closing a savings account within 12 months of opening costs ₹500. After 12 months, closure is
free. The customer must return unused cheque leaves and the debit card.
